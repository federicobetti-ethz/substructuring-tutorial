Substructuring tutorial
=======================

Plane-stress Craig–Bampton and Rubin reduction of a cantilever, as used in the
tutorials of the Chair of Structural Mechanics and Monitoring at ETH Zürich.

The beam is cut into a chain of components. Each component is replaced by a
small set of constraint or attachment modes and a few vibration modes, the
components are coupled at the cuts, and the tip motion is compared with the
unreduced finite-element model.
